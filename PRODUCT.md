# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Hospital and clinic administrators managing daily care operations. They handle care requests, provider coordination, patient records, billing, and reporting. Context: busy, multitasking, need fast access to critical information without friction.

## Product Purpose

Care Now is a healthcare operations dashboard that streamlines care management. It centralizes requests, providers, patients, billing, and analytics in one interface. Success: admins complete workflows quickly without switching tools or losing context.

## Brand Personality

**Professional, calm, confident.**

Reference: Linear (clean, confident, no noise), Stripe (polished, trustworthy, precise). Voice is clear and direct — never playful, never clinical. Avoid clutter, visual noise, and unnecessary decoration.

## Anti-references

Legacy EHR systems (Epic, Cerner): cluttered interfaces, outdated aesthetics, poor UX, information overload, confusing navigation. This product should feel modern, clean, and intentional — the opposite of legacy healthcare software.

## Design Principles

1. **Clarity over density** — show what matters, hide what doesn't. Every pixel earns its place.
2. **Confident simplicity** — trust the user; don't over-explain or over-decorate.
3. **Workflow-first** — optimize for task completion, not visual appeal.
4. **Consistent rhythm** — predictable patterns reduce cognitive load.
5. **Accessible by default** — WCAG 2.1 AAA; high contrast, clear hierarchy, keyboard-navigable.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.1 AAA compliance required. High contrast ratios, clear focus indicators, keyboard navigation, screen reader support. Consider reduced motion preferences and color blindness accommodations.
